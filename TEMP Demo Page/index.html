03 · SMAYA simulator

Watch the SMAYA box package your data.

Sensors on the cooling tower feed the edge box. The box bundles every reading into a signed packet, holds it safely if the connection drops, and sends it on to the dashboard, where it turns into savings, predictions and actions. Move the sliders or trigger a scenario.

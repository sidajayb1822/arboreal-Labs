The shift

Stop running a deal firm out of spreadsheets and chat groups

Most advisory teams track deals in a shared sheet, chase investors over chat and keep the real status in someone's head. Leads go cold, the same fund gets pitched twice, and nobody can say how healthy a mandate really is until it is too late.

Before

  • Founder enquiries arrive by email, forms and messages, with no single list
  • Fundraise status lives in a sheet that is out of date by Friday
  • Follow-ups depend on someone remembering to do them
  • Partner referrals and fees are worked out by hand at the end

After

  • Every enquiry lands on one board through your own shareable forms
  • Each fund's status, contact route and history sits on the deal itself
  • Moving a deal forward raises the right tasks automatically
  • Partner credit and earnings are calculated for you, deal by deal
From first enquiry to closed round

From first enquiry to closed round

The platform carries a company through each step and handles the busywork in between.

  1. CaptureFounders apply through branded forms shared by link or QR code, and each submission is credited to whoever shared it.
  2. ScreenEntries land on a pipeline board where the team moves them through your own stages, with automatic tasks raised at each one.
  3. AcceptAccepting an entry opens a live deal and adds the company to your database without anyone retyping it.
  4. FundraiseBuild the investor list, let the founder approve it, then track every fund from first contact to diligence with a live health score.
  5. CloseRecord commitments, funds received and shares transferred, and the system works out each partner's share of the fee.

One Platform, Three Pillars

Find the deals, run the deals, and run the firm behind them.

Source

Forms & Pipelines

Build intake forms on a visual canvas with branching questions and an "eligible / not eligible" ending. Every team member and partner gets their own link and QR code with submission counts. Submissions arrive on a drag-and-drop board with Lead, Accepted and Rejected stages plus any stages you add.

Deal Desk

Associates import deal cards in bulk or log a company in seconds, and record call notes, including voice notes. Founders review each associate's desk and mark deals Rejected, Discuss in person or More info requested.

Referrals & Partner Desk

Every staff member has a personal referral link, and leaders see how many companies each one has brought in. Partners log companies they find; a coordinator picks the next step and assigns it, which becomes a real task the partner can follow.

Companies

The record of every startup you meet: founders, team, cap table, funding rounds, documents and an update timeline. Each profile suggests investors by sector, by synergy and from sector-agnostic funds.

Execute

Active Deals

Each deal shows its round: the total raise, what outside investors have already filled, the minimum ticket and what is still open, plus valuation on a pre- or post-money basis. Investor commitments move from Not started to Commitment received, Funds received and Shares transferred.

Fundraise Tracker

Track every fund on a mandate across 17 statuses, from first contact and warm introductions through Deal sent, Data requested and Fund due diligence. Funds that go quiet are flagged as ghosted automatically. A health score rates each mandate Strong, Steady, Thin or Stalled, and the founder gets a private status link.

Investor CRM

A fund database with sectors, ticket sizes in ₹ or $, stage focus, contacts and owners on your team. Investor lists are shared with the founder for approval, and a separate angel outreach flow handles syndicate deals. Every edit is logged.

Client Projects

Paid work such as pitch decks, projections and valuation reports runs through eight stages, from Lead to Completed. The proposal cart works out 18% GST and the 50% advance, a data checklist must be complete before work starts, and clients follow progress on a read-only link.

Operate

Tasks & KPIs

A shared board with Board, List and By-person views, comments, alerts and recurring templates. Assignees can push a due date while the original is kept. A KPI view shows each person's on-time, pushed, pending and missed tasks, and a weekly update is compiled from the work.

Partner Programme

Partners get their own portal with their links, the submissions those links brought in, and their earnings per deal. Referral chains roll credit up to the partner who started them. Two partner tiers decide whether a partner sees every shared deal or only the ones they are named on.

HR & People

Leave and expense requests go through an approvals queue with live leave balances. Monthly attendance statements, a policy library and an events calendar with RSVPs sit alongside. HR can issue 25 kinds of letter on company letterhead, each with a QR code a bank or landlord can scan to verify it.

Analytics & Recognition

Leaders see a performance score per person built from on-time tasks, recurring work, event attendance and kudos, with weightings they can adjust. Teammates thank each other on a public kudos feed, and escalations move from Open to Acknowledged to Resolved.

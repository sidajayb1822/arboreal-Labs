Last updated: 25 June 2026

Arboreal Labs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working correctly and to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some functionality.

These providers process data only on our behalf and in line with their own privacy terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites we link to.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, or as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

6. How We Protect Your Data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Platform stock movements and records are maintained as auditable transactions. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be communicated where appropriate.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out at founders@arboreallabs.com or through our contact page.

From first call to order

How a Lead Becomes an Order

Six steps take a lead from first contact to purchase order, and the system keeps the record as you go.

  1. CaptureAdd a marketing lead with its source, location and as many contacts as you need, and the system opens a timeline for it.
  2. NurtureLog calls, emails and meetings, and set a next step with a due date that appears on the team's task list automatically.
  3. QualifyMove the lead through eight stages, and every change is time-stamped; reaching Presentation counts towards each person's weekly and monthly targets.
  4. Hand overMove the lead to Sales in one click; the marketing record locks and the full history comes across.
  5. CloseWork through seven milestones from Site Survey to Order Received while the deal value updates from the latest figure.
  6. ReviewDashboards add up closed revenue, pipeline value, conversions and lost-deal reasons, measured against yearly targets.

Six Workspaces, One Pipeline

Find leads, close deals and run the team: three pillars that share one live record of every customer.

Find

Marketing Leads

A searchable directory of every lead, shown as cards or a list. Each lead moves through eight stages from Lead Generated to Closed-Won. Marking a lead Interest Dropped, Shelved or Closed-Lost asks for a reason first. Search several terms at once by separating them with commas.

Activity Timeline

Every call, email, meeting, status change and handover is recorded with who did it and when. Lead sources and interaction types build up from what your team enters, so nobody has to maintain a master list.

Close

Sales Pipeline

Every qualified deal in one list showing stage, value, owner, latest interaction and days open. The page totals the active amount as you search. Deals open for more than 65 days are flagged in red. Export the whole pipeline to a spreadsheet in one click.

Deal Milestones

Seven milestones: Site Survey, Budgetary Offer, Budget Available, Technical Clarity, PR Raised, Negotiation and Order Received. Each offer revision is kept separately, and every reminder sent after a PR is raised is logged. Deal value always reflects the latest figure, and deals can be shelved with a reason or reopened later.

Run the Team

Planning and Tasks

All next steps from all leads in one list, sorted by due date. Overdue items are flagged. Tasks finished late ask for a reason, and late finishes are counted per person. Filter by teammate or date range, and add standalone tasks that aren't tied to a customer.

Minutes of Meeting

Record each meeting's attendees, notes and action items, and tag teammates with @mentions. Tick off action items as they're done and see progress at a glance. Search past meetings, and turn any of them into a print-ready PDF.

Performance Analytics

Marketing view: interactions, active leads, a status-by-source chart, where leads lose interest, and late tasks per person. Sales view: closed revenue, active pipeline value, a funnel of live deals, and the most common reasons deals are lost.

Users and Targets

Admins add or remove team members, set yearly group targets and see every person's progress in one table. Employees set their own weekly and monthly goals and can update contacts, but only admins can rename, re-source or delete leads.

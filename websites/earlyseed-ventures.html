Our story

How this site came together

  1. The brief

    Two audiences, one mission

    Earlyseed speaks to founders looking for the right investors and to investors looking for the right companies. The site had to welcome both, and carry the firm's mission of a self-reliant India.

  2. The partnership

    Working alongside Hereafter

    We partnered with Hereafter on the project, bringing Earlyseed's warm, editorial look to the web.

  3. How we built it

    A path for founders, a path for investors

    Separate pages for founders and investors explain what Earlyseed does for each, alongside the team, the portfolio, the syndicate, the hub and testimonials from the founders they've worked with.

  4. Launch

    Live on earlyseedventures.com

    The site is live, giving founders and investors one place to understand how Earlyseed works.

  5. Since launch

    Content, managed by us

    We keep the site current, adding new portfolio companies, team updates and hub articles as they come.

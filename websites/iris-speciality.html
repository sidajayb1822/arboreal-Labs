Our story

How this site came together

  1. The brief

    Premium materials, presented clearly

    Iris imports raw materials and specialty chemicals for paint and ink makers. Their buyers are technical, so the site needed to show each product plainly and make it easy to get in touch.

  2. The partnership

    Working alongside Hereafter

    We partnered with Hereafter on the project, bringing Iris's brand colours and product range together on the web.

  3. How we built it

    Every product, one enquiry away

    Each product, from resins and emulsions to pigments and special effect finishes, gets a short description and its own enquiry button, so buyers go from browsing to asking in one step.

  4. Launch

    Live on irisspeciality.com

    The site is live, giving paint and ink manufacturers across India one clear place to explore Iris's range.

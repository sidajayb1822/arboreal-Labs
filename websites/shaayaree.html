Our story

How this site came together

  1. The brief

    A portfolio worthy of the art form

    Shaayaree performs at temple festivals, cultural events, weddings and on stage. Her site had to feel as graceful as her dance, while making it simple for organisers to see her work and book her.

  2. What we found

    Years of training, hard to see at a glance

    Her degree, guru lineage, distinctions and performances were spread across different places. Visitors needed one place to understand her journey.

  3. How we built it

    Rich, classical and easy to book

    Deep maroon and gold set a classical stage. The site tells her story through education, guru lineage, distinctions, a gallery, performance videos and her repertoire, with a Book Now button always in reach.

  4. Launch

    Her stage, online

    The site is live, giving organisers one place to watch her perform and get in touch.

  5. Since launch

    SEO and content, managed by us

    We keep her site easy to find on search and add new performances, videos and achievements as they happen.

Our story

How this site came together

  1. The brief

    A portfolio you explore, not scroll

    Our friend didn't want a standard portfolio. He wanted visitors to actually move through his own little world and discover his work as they go.

  2. The idea

    Turn the portfolio into a place

    We designed it like a small game: a pixel-art planet floating in space, with islands and landmarks that each hold a part of his story.

  3. How we built it

    A world you can click into

    The site opens on the planet with a single prompt: click to enter. From there, visitors traverse the world at their own pace, so every visit feels like exploring rather than reading.

  4. Launch

    Live as Siddhant's World

    The finished world is live, and it's one of the concepts we like to show when a client wants a site that people remember.

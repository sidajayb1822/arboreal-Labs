Our story

How this site came together

  1. The brief

    On brand, with the full range on show

    The company had clear brand guidelines and a wide product range. They wanted a site that followed those guidelines closely and made it easy for buyers to see every product and where it's used.

  2. What we found

    A big range that needed structure

    With products for two- and three-wheelers, forklifts, marine and energy storage, the challenge was presenting a lot of detail without overwhelming visitors.

  3. How we built it

    Brand first, products organised by use

    We built every page around their colours, type and imagery, then organised the product range by the industry it serves, with key figures and a specification sheet for each product.

  4. Launch

    Live on webberec.com

    The site is live, giving buyers and partners one clear place to explore the company, its products and the industries it powers.
